What it does

Paint, then ask

Brush over the sofa, the worktop, the blank wall. Then describe what should be there instead.

For small fixes

When the shot is already right apart from one thing, this is faster than staging the room again.

One area at a time

One mask, one instruction. For several areas at once, each with its own furniture, use the Masking Studio.

Everything outside the stroke comes back untouched Not re-rendered, not nudged, not re-lit.
