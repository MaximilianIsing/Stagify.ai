About Stagify.ai

Stagify.ai is an AI virtual staging tool for real estate professionals. Upload a photo of a room, and get it furnished, redesigned or cleared in seconds.

Who we are

Stagify.ai is an independent company. We build and operate exactly one product, and it lives at one address: stagify.ai. We are not a reseller, a white-label of another staging tool, or a regional branch of a larger group. You can reach the whole team at team@stagify.ai or on (929) 251-4372.

What we make

Stagify.ai stages interiors from a single photo, repaints and refurnishes rooms through a conversational designer, masks and restages individual areas pixel by pixel, and cleans up exterior shots. It runs in the browser in eleven languages, with a free tier, a Stagify+ subscription and a public API. You keep full rights to every image you generate.
