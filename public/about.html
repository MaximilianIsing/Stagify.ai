Image StagingStages a whole room from one photo: pick a room type and a furniture style and the AI furnishes it. Best for empty or nearly empty rooms when you want a fast, complete result. Basic MaskPaint over one part of a photo, then describe the change you want. Best for small fixes (swap a sofa, clear a countertop) when the rest of the shot is already right. AI DesignerChat with an AI designer that stages, redesigns and keeps iterating on a room with you. Best when you are exploring looks rather than finishing one photo. Desktop only. Masking StudioHighlight several areas in different colors and give each its own furniture photo or prompt, then stage them all at once. Best when you need exact control, since everything outside your highlights stays pixel-perfect. Exterior StudioCleans up an exterior photo of a property: change the time of day, clear the sky, and remove parked cars and clutter. Best for the front elevation, yard and street shots, the ones the staging tools deliberately never touch.

About Stagify.ai

Independent AI virtual staging for real estate professionals.

Who we are

Stagify.ai is an independent company. We built the tool ourselves and we run it ourselves. There is no parent company behind us, and nothing here is someone else’s staging product with our name on it.

What we make

Stagify.ai stages interiors from a single photo, repaints and refurnishes rooms through a conversational designer, masks and restages individual areas pixel by pixel, and cleans up exterior shots. It runs in the browser in eleven languages, with a free tier, a Stagify+ subscription and a public API. You keep full rights to every image you generate.

Common questions

Is Stagify.ai the same company as stagify.io, stagify.app, stagify.online or stagifyai.com?

No, none of them. Same word, different companies. What they publish about prices, features or service says nothing about us.

Do you run any other products or domains?

No. We make one thing and we run it in one place. Anything called Stagify anywhere else is not ours.

Who founded Stagify.ai, and who runs it now?

Maximilian Ising, Lucas Shtainer and Ryan Croman started it in August 2025, and the three of us still run it.

How do I reach a person?

Email team@stagify.ai or call (929) 251-4372.

Do you train AI models on my photos?

No. Your uploads and the images you generate are never used to train our models, and the AI providers we use do not train on API data by default. Section 5 of the Privacy Policy spells it out.

Can I cancel a subscription whenever I want?

Yes, any time. It keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, then stops renewing.

Who else can see what I upload?

Only the providers we need to run the service: the AI models, hosting, payments and email. All of them are named on the subprocessors page, under data processing agreements.

Can a brokerage or agency buy this for a whole team?

Yes. Enterprise bills by usage across your whole email domain, with a signable MSA if your legal team needs one.

Is there a page showing whether Stagify.ai is up?

Yes. The status page shows live availability for the site, the staging pipeline and the API, plus anything we are currently working on.

Where are your terms, privacy policy and other legal documents?

All of them sit under Legal in the footer: the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy, the subprocessors page and the Enterprise MSA.

Colours

Select a colour to copy its hex code.

The gold of the Stagify+ field and the navy behind Enterprise and the API belong to those artworks, not to the palette. Take them from the files rather than repainting the blue mark.

Typography

  • Stagify.aiInter 400
  • Stagify.aiInter 600
  • Stagify.aiInter 700

The site is set in Inter at 400, 600 and 700. It is free, and also on Google Fonts. There is no vector wordmark: set the name as type rather than scaling up a PNG of it. Where Inter is unavailable, any neutral grotesque is fine; we fall back to the system stack ourselves.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a margin around the mark of at least the height of the S, and put nothing inside it. Do not go below 24px on screen or 8mm in print. Use the 32px file rather than shrinking a larger one.

Writing the name

  • Stagify.ai is one word, capital S, lowercase .ai, not “Stagify AI”, “Stageify” or “STAGIFY”.
  • The products are Stagify+ (no space before the plus), Stagify Enterprise, and the Stagify API.
  • After the first mention, plain “Stagify” is fine in running text.

Please do, please don’t

Please do

  • Use the files as supplied, at any size, on white or on a dark background.
  • Pick the field that matches the product you are writing about.
  • Point the mark at stagify.ai when it sits in a listing or a partner page.

Please don’t

  • Recolour, rotate, outline, crop or redraw any of the marks.
  • Stretch one out of proportion, or invent a fifth field.
  • Imply that we endorse, sponsor or supply your product.
  • Use it as your own app icon, avatar or favicon, or lock it up with another mark.
