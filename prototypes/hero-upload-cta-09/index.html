Hero CTA — eight styles, houses scaled to the door

Fix list from review: door too big on Luxury / Scandinavian / Standard · Standard’s lamp covered · too many windows on Custom · a door is one storey, scale houses to it · the right-hand house must move with the rest of the parallax · Farmhouse doesn’t look like a farmhouse · far backgrounds should differ per style.

  • Scale. The entrance is rendered at 38 × 36 (was 46 × 44) and every house is rebuilt on a 22-unit storey grid: ground floor y 44–66, upper floor y 22–44, roof above. The door leaf is ≈ 19 units tall — one storey. Windows are 8 × 10.
  • Parallax. The entrance is now drawn inside the scene SVG as part of the house, so the whole house — door included — pans in the mid band with the street. Nothing is static any more.
  • Custom. Brownstone cut to two upper windows and one ground-floor window either side of the stoop.
  • Standard. Coach lamp moved to the top-left of the porch, clear of the door and the flanking window.
  • Farmhouse. Redrawn: white board-and-batten, standing-seam metal porch roof on square posts, cross-buck door with four lights, hanging lantern, planter — and a red barn, silo and treeline behind it.
  • Far backgrounds. Each style has its own: UK terrace roofline (Standard), city skyline (Modern), pine forest and a mountain (Scandinavian), sea, lighthouse and boats (Coastal), fields, barn and silo (Farmhouse), the Enterprise city with a dome (Luxury), desert hills and palms (Midcentury), a bridge and towers (Custom).
