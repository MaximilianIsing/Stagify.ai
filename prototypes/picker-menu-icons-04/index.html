01

Peel

C 75%

The sticker lifts and tilts under the pointer. Carried forward as one baseline.

  • One transform on the shipped sticker, no new art
  • The resting tilt marks the selected row without a fill
  • Nothing behind the sticker, so the row has no anchor
  • Touch never sees the lift
  • A scaled sticker can clip the row above on tight menus
02

Die-cut Halo

C 74%

The sticker on a pale blob of its material colour. Carried forward as the other baseline.

  • The blob gives every row an anchor and a hue to scan by
  • Backing survives the selected row where fills cannot
  • Nothing rewards the pointer
  • One more colour per item to keep on brand
  • Fourteen hues can read as a candy shelf
03

Halo Peel

B+ 89%

The straight mix: the blob stays put, the sticker peels off it under the pointer.

  • The blob is the spot the sticker visibly lifts away from
  • Both halves already exist, this is one class on each row
  • Blob and tilt together mark the selected row twice over
  • Gates behind prefers-reduced-motion with the blob still shown
  • Touch gets the blob but never the peel
  • Fourteen filtered transforms on a fast sweep costs frames
  • Lifted sticker can overlap the row above at 1x zoom
04

Squash

B 84%

The blob flattens like a cushion as the sticker rises straight off it. Weight, not spin.

  • The blob reacting to the lift sells the sticker as a thing
  • Vertical lift only, so nothing rotates into the neighbour row
  • Selected row gets a white ring on the blob, visible on navy
  • Two transitions per row instead of one
  • Squash reads as a bounce on slower machines
  • Straight lift loses the tilt that said "sticker" in the first place
05

Wobble

C+ 79%

The blob changes shape and turns one way while the sticker tilts the other.

  • The most alive of the family, the blob feels like liquid
  • Counter-rotation makes the lift read even at small size
  • Border-radius morph is cheap, no filter change
  • Two things moving in opposite directions is busy on a sweep
  • Morphing blobs read as loading spinners to some visitors
  • Harder to tune: four radii and two rotations per state
06

Ring

C- 72%

The halo as a dashed die-cut line in the accent colour, turning slightly as the sticker peels.

  • Lightest of the family, the menu stays mostly white
  • The dashed line rhymes with the cut edge of the sticker
  • Selected row turns the ring white, clean on navy
  • A dashed line is not an anchor, the row loses the blob's mass
  • 1.6px dashes alias on 1x displays
  • Least pull of the eight by a distance
07

Spotlight

B- 82%

Blobs rest in neutral wash; the pointer brings the colour, and the chosen row keeps it.

  • Resting menu stays inside the blue-and-white system
  • Colour becomes a signal, only the hovered and chosen rows carry it
  • Same peel, so the pointer gets two rewards for one hover
  • Touch sees colour on the selected row only, the rest stay grey
  • Scanning by hue no longer works at rest
  • Three properties transition per row on a sweep
08

Burst

D 62%

The swing: hovering pops the sticker and throws a ring of confetti out of the blob.

  • Nobody forgets it
  • Pure CSS, six radial gradients and two keyframes
  • Delightful once, tiresome by the third hover
  • Confetti overhangs neighbouring rows and the popover edge
  • An animation per row on a sweep is a frame-rate cost
  • Reads as a game, not a tool agents pay for
