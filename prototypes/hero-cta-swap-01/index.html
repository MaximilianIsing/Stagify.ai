Hero CTA style swap: animation directions

"You know how for the 'Stage this {room} in {style}' on the home page the background for the upload image for free button changes? I don't like how it instantly changes so abruptly, I'd like there to be a nice animation."

21 September 2026. The street inside the CTA changes house per style; today the SVG is swapped in one frame. Pick a style chip in any card to play its swap.

Ranking

Scored on fit to the brief (30), distinctiveness (20), build cost (20), mobile and accessibility (15), first-glance pull (15).

  1. 02Street Pan 90%A-

    Reuses the shipped parallax bands, reads as one camera move, never touches the van logic.

  2. 05Van Delivery 88%B+

    Best story, ties the swap to the moving-day van, but collides with the hover van.

  3. 03Lights Out 84%B

    Hides the hard cut inside a blink; the window flicker is the charm.

  4. 04Sink and Rise 80%B-

    Playful and clear, but a second longer than the others and needs a ground clip.

  5. 06Door Iris 79%C+

    One clip-path line, but the circle reads as generic UI rather than a street.

  6. 08Blueprint Draw 75%C

    On brand for a staging tool, yet 1.5 seconds and fragile across eight house drawings.

  7. 01Soft Dissolve 73%C

    Fixes the abruptness in ten lines, and that is all it does.

  8. 07Split Flap 65%D

    Memorable, but a departures board is the wrong metaphor for a street scene.

01

Soft Dissolve

C 73%

Safest fix: a half-second crossfade between the two streets, nothing else moves.

  • Ten lines of CSS on the existing scene span
  • Zero risk to the hover van timeline
  • Works unchanged under reduced motion
  • Two houses ghost through each other mid-fade
  • Reads as a slideshow, not a place
  • Earns no attention for the style pick
02

Street Pan

A- 90%

The camera dollies down the street to the next house; sky stays, bands slide at their parallax depths.

  • Same far, mid, home, near bands the hover already pans
  • Tells a story: every style lives on one street
  • Under one second, no overlap ghosting
  • Pure transform, cheap to paint
  • Needs two scenes mounted for one second
  • Rapid chip clicks must cancel cleanly
  • Reduced motion needs a dissolve fallback
03

Lights Out

B 84%

The street blacks out, the house changes in the dark, then windows and lantern flick back on.

  • The swap itself stays a one-frame innerHTML
  • Flicker reuses the existing lit window class
  • Fits the night scene naturally
  • Plate goes fully dark, label contrast dips briefly
  • Blink can read as a glitch on fast machines
  • Nine staggered animations to tune per house
04

Sink and Rise

B- 80%

Old houses sink under the road with a dust puff; the new ones rise and settle with a bounce.

  • Only the houses move, sky and road stay put
  • Clear cause and effect for the chip click
  • Playful without new artwork
  • About 1.2 seconds, slowest of the safe picks
  • Needs a ground clip path on both bands
  • Stagger timings drift across eight house heights
05

Van Delivery

B+ 88%

The removal van drives across the plate and drags the new street in behind it, a masked wipe.

  • Reuses the van the hover already draws
  • Strongest story: the movers bring the new house
  • Highest pull, people will click chips to watch it
  • Collides with a hover van already parked
  • Needs a registered property or a rAF loop
  • 1.3 seconds, feels slow on the fifth click
06

Door Iris

C+ 79%

The new street opens as a circle from the front door, with a gold ring pulse.

  • One animated clip-path, nothing inside the SVG changes
  • Anchored on the door, the hover's focal point
  • Fast, 0.75 seconds
  • Circle wipe reads as a generic app transition
  • Door position differs per house, anchor drifts
  • Ring pulse fights the lantern glow on hover
07

Split Flap

D 65%

The plate flips like an airport departures board; the top half falls to reveal the next street.

  • Instantly memorable, nobody else does this
  • Mechanical timing makes the swap feel deliberate
  • Wrong metaphor: a board, not a street
  • Four scene copies mounted per swap
  • 3D transforms and the hover camera scale conflict
  • Label is cut in half mid-flip
08

Blueprint Draw

C 75%

The old house fades, the new one is drawn as a blue line sketch, then inked in.

  • Says "designed", on brand for virtual staging
  • Only the house bands are touched
  • Distinct from every other transition on the site
  • 1.5 seconds, longest here
  • Dash lengths must be tuned per house path
  • Stroke on every child hits filled windows oddly
  • Heavy paint on large plates
