Instagram follow CTA — plate directions

Request: “Something kinda like the View Stagify+ / View Stagify Enterprise button, where there’s something cool in the background and something cool happens when you hover.”

Stagify.ai · prototypes/ig-follow-button-02 · 7 plates + today’s button for reference · generated 2026-09-20

Every plate below is built on the exact .plus-cta.plus-plate geometry from public/styles/home.css — 14/16px padding, 12px radius, 15.5px/800 label, a 30px glyph well, a 2px hover lift, and the same two scene layers (::before = the world, ::after = what’s in it) with the 1.2s parallax easing. The scenes are inline SVG data URIs, same as the rooms and the skyline. So each of these is a drop-in re-skin of .ig-follow, not a new component — and each ships its own prefers-reduced-motion resting state, which this page honours too. Hover every one.

01

Endless Feed

The safest pick, and the closest sibling to Stagify+. Same --cta-gradient sky, so the hierarchy is untouched; the scene is a tiling wall of posts — tinted covers with tiny sofas, beds and lamps on a faint grid. It says “there is a lot more of this” without a single word of copy. Tradeoff: it is the least surprising of the seven.

On hover · the whole wall scrolls up by exactly one tile — a feed that never ends

Shutter

Pick this if the button should read “camera” before it reads “social network”. A six-bladed aperture sits off the right edge over a plum-and-magenta sky — the only non-blue plate in the set, which is exactly why it stands out after six rows of brand blue. Tradeoff: it breaks the site’s colour discipline, so it needs a deliberate yes.

Stories

The most literally “Instagram” of the seven. A row of story bubbles, each ringed in Instagram’s own sunset ramp, on a night-navy sky with a vignette that keeps the label readable. The ramp appears only as 2.6px rings — enough to be recognised, not enough to hijack the page’s colour. Tradeoff: borrowed visual language; it will date if Instagram restyles rings.

Empty → Staged · the recommendation

Pick this one. It is the only plate whose hover is the product rather than an effect. At rest the scene is an empty isometric room on the Stagify+ sky — floor, walls, nothing in it. Hover, and a sofa, coffee table, TV wall, floor lamp and plant spring up into place while the camera pushes in and the neighbouring grid pans the other way. It matches the Stagify+ plate’s family exactly (same gradient, same 26.57° iso grid, same parallax), so the three buttons read as one system, and it earns the Instagram section instead of decorating it.

Double-Tap

Pick this if the plate should be alive even when nobody touches it. Likes drift upward through a magenta-to-navy sky on a slow 14s loop — the only plate here with resting motion. It also carries a live count next to the label. Tradeoff: ambient motion on a marketing page is a real decision, and the count is content someone has to keep honest.

06

Dive In

The biggest single gesture in the set. At rest it is a tiny 3×3 profile grid, nine covers, unmistakably an Instagram page. Hover, and the grid rushes toward you and dissolves into one full staged room — you have fallen into a post. Tradeoff: it is a 3.6× scale on a pseudo-element, so it is the one to check on a low-end laptop before shipping.

On hover · the grid zooms 3.6× and cross-fades into the post behind it

In The App

Pick this if you want the button to show the destination. A small tilted phone sits on the plate with a real feed inside it, clipped by the handset — and on hover the phone straightens up, comes toward you, and the feed scrolls under your cursor. It is the only variant with a child element rather than pure pseudo-layers, because the clip has to be a real overflow boundary. Tradeoff: one extra span in the markup and a phone drawn in CSS that has to stay crisp at 1× and 2×.

On hover · the phone straightens and scales up while its feed scrolls half a screen
