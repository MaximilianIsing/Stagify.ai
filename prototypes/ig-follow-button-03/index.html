Instagram follow plate — generation 3

Brief: “05 is the closest thing. But I don’t want to show a live number. I also find the hearts weird and unnatural — do something more specific to Instagram.”

Stagify.ai · prototypes/ig-follow-button-03 · 6 plates · generated 2026-09-20

All six keep what worked about gen-2’s Double-Tap: the magenta → violet → navy sky, and a background that is already moving before you touch it. What’s gone: the follower count (no number anywhere on this page) and the floating heart swarm — hearts drifting up out of nothing was the unnatural part, since Instagram has no such thing. Each plate instead borrows one motion that actually exists in the app — the gradient, the camera mark, a carousel swipe, a reel scroll, the double-tap pop, a story bar — so the movement is recognised rather than invented. Same .plus-plate geometry as ever; every one is a drop-in for .ig-follow. Hover each one.

01

Aurora

The quietest of the six, and the hardest to get tired of. No objects at all — the Instagram gradient is the scene. Three colour blobs drift across each other on a 22-second loop, so the plate is never the same twice, and nothing on it can look fake because nothing on it is a thing. Tradeoff: it’s abstract, so it says “Instagram” only through colour.

Glyph Field

Pick this if you want it unmistakable at a glance. The Instagram camera mark, tiled edge to edge at 20% opacity and creeping diagonally, with two larger near-field glyphs drifting the other way. It reads as Instagram from two metres away without using a single word of Meta’s colour. Tradeoff: a repeating logo is a pattern, not a picture — it has nothing to say beyond “Instagram”.

03

Carousel

The most Instagram-literate motion there is. The plate holds a post — a staged room, a lamp, a plant — with carousel dots along the bottom edge, and hovering swipes to the next slide and steps the dot. Everyone has made that exact gesture ten thousand times, so it needs no explaining. Tradeoff: at rest it is a still image, so it has less ambient life than 01 or 05.

Reels

Pick this if the account leans on video. A column of vertical reel thumbnails rolls slowly up the right-hand side behind a play button that breathes on a 3.4s beat. The vertical scroll is the one motion nothing else on the homepage does. Tradeoff: it promises reels — if the account is mostly stills, the button is writing a cheque the feed doesn’t cash.

Double-Tap · the direct rewrite of gen-2 05

The same idea you liked, done the way Instagram actually does it. Not a swarm — one heart, the double-tap heart, which pops onto the photo with the overshoot and the ring exactly as the app plays it, then leaves. Behind it is a real staged room with a slow 24-second push-in, so the plate breathes at rest without anything floating around on it. No counts, no badges, no numbers.

06

Story

The one that makes the button feel live. Four segmented story bars run along the top edge; the first one is always filling, so the plate is permanently mid-story — the strongest “this account posts” signal on the page, and it makes that claim without printing a number. Hovering taps forward: bar 1 completes, the photo cuts to the next story, bar 2 starts running. Tradeoff: the bars are 2.5px of chrome borrowed from the app, and they only read correctly if people recognise them.
