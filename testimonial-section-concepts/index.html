“What agents are saying”

Seven ways to rebuild the home page’s testimonials section for six quotes instead of two, plus today’s version as the baseline. Every concept renders into the same 1044px content box (.home-section is 1140px wide with 48px side padding), so the height badges are a fair comparison.

All six testimonials are fictional placeholders — invented people at invented brokerages, written at the length of the two real quotes (~35–45 words) so the layouts are stressed honestly. Logos are generated monograms in assets/, at the real 72×72 geometry. Concept 6’s room photos are marked STAND-IN: they are the three real Homepage/BeforeAfter/After*.webp renders cycled twice, not six different listings. Read README.md for what each concept costs to ship.

Snap rail cheapest

One horizontal rail with scroll snapping. Two and a bit cards show at desktop width, one and a bit on a phone — the same markup and the same CSS at both sizes, so there is no second layout to maintain. The half-visible third card is what tells you to keep scrolling. Native scroll-snap-type does the scrolling; JS only paints the dots and moves focus.

  • One code path for desktop and phone
  • Scrolls natively — works before JS loads
  • Adding a seventh quote costs nothing
  • Quotes past the second need a deliberate scroll
  • Least surprising of the seven

Featured + roster shortest

One quote on stage at a time, with the six brokerage logos below acting as the selector. The section is the height of a single quote no matter how many you have, and the logo row does double duty as a “trusted by” band — the proof lands even if nobody clicks. Wall of six

All six at once in a 3×3 mosaic with the strongest quote promoted to a 2×2 hero cell — 4 + 5 = 9 cells, an exact fit. Nothing to click, nothing hidden, no JS at desktop width; the impression of volume is the whole point, and you cannot get that from a widget that shows one quote at a time. It is the tallest option here.

  • Every quote visible — volume is the message
  • No JS at all above 768px
  • Reads well when screenshotted for an ad
  • Tallest concept here, between two heavy sections
  • A seventh quote breaks the 3×2 rhythm

4

Counter-marquee

Two rows ticking past in opposite directions, pausing on hover or keyboard focus. The section is a fixed height forever — a seventh, or a twentieth, testimonial costs no layout at all. It is the same idiom as the sponsor logo marquee two sections up, which is either pleasing repetition or a repeat, depending on taste.

  • Fixed height regardless of how many quotes exist
  • Motion draws the eye without a click
  • Reuses the shipped sponsors-scroll.js idiom
  • Moving text is hard to finish reading
  • Cards must be duplicated — watch the a11y tree
  • Second marquee on one page

5

Swipe deck

The six quotes as a physical stack, slightly fanned, with the top card draggable off to reveal the next. The most distinctive thing on this page, and the most expensive: drag handling, a counter, buttons, and a keyboard story that has to be built rather than inherited. Included so the ambitious end of the range is represented honestly.

  • Nothing else on the site looks like this
  • Compact — one card’s height
  • The stack itself says “there are more”
  • Most JS of the seven, all of it bespoke
  • Drag affordance is invisible until tried
  • Weakest keyboard and screen-reader story

Editorial + rating band recommended

One quote set as a full-width pull quote, big enough to actually read, rotating on a timer with a progress bar you can pause. Under it, the number that a card grid can never state: 5.0 from 6 reviews, with the six logos as a trust strip. It is the only concept that publishes an aggregate, which is also the hook for AggregateRating structured data — the repo has none today, and that is the review stars Google shows in search results.

  • Typographic scale makes one section feel premium
  • States an aggregate; unlocks review-star rich results
  • Compact, and pairs with the existing Google-review CTA
  • Logo strip proves breadth without a grid
  • Long quotes at 28px need a length ceiling
  • Auto-advance needs pause rules done properly
