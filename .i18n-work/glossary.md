# Stagify blog translation glossary

Binding terminology. These are not suggestions — they are the words the Stagify UI already
uses in each language (`public/languages/<lang>.json`). An article that invents its own term
reads as a different product from the app it is advertising.

## "virtual staging" — the product category

| lang | term to use |
|------|-------------|
| es | home staging virtual |
| fr | home staging virtuel |
| de | virtuelles Home Staging |
| zh | 虚拟软装 |
| ko | 버추얼 스테이징 |
| pt | home staging virtual |
| ru | виртуальный хоум-стейджинг |
| it | home staging virtuale |
| ja | バーチャルステージング |
| nl | virtuele woningstyling |

Inflect naturally (plural, adjectival, verb forms). On later mentions a shortened form is
fine where the language allows it — "staging", "стейджинг", "ステージング", "styling" — as
long as the first mention uses the full term above.

## Never translated

Stagify, Stagify.ai, Stagify+, Masking Studio, AI Designer, Exterior Studio, Basic Mask,
MLS, NAR, FSBO, RESA, Zillow, Redfin.

These are product and organization names. They appear in English in the app's own UI in
every language, so translating them here would name a product the reader cannot find.

You MAY gloss an acronym on first use where the reader would not know it — e.g. "MLS (the
US multiple listing service)" — but the acronym itself stays.

## Numbers, money, dates

- Prices are US dollars and stay in dollars: `$1,500`, `$11.99`. Never convert a currency
  and never invent a local price.
- Apply the target language's own number and decimal formatting where it differs.
- Dates: render in the language's conventional long form. Never change the date itself.
- US statistics (NAR, RESA figures, "83% of buyers' agents") keep their numbers and their
  attribution exactly. Do not localize a statistic — it describes the US market.

## Register

Direct, concrete, confident. Short sentences. No filler, no throat-clearing, no
"in today's fast-paced real estate market". Match the English sentence for sentence in
substance; do not pad and do not summarize.
