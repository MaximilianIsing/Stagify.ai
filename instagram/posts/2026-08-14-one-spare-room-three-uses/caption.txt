Every house has one room nobody can name. The listing photo has to call it something, and whatever you call it is what the buyer prices.

Empty, that room is a question. We staged the same photograph three times and never moved the camera.

As an office it sells to the buyer who works from home. As a guest room it sells to the buyer whose family visits. As a reading room it sells to the buyer who wants somewhere quiet. Same walls, same window, same floor. Three different buyers.

Go and look at your own gallery. If a room in it has no furniture and no label, the buyer is filling it in for you, and they will fill it in with the version they do not want.
