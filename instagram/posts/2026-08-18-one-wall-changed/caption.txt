We brushed one wall. The bath, the tap and the towels are the same pixels they were before.

That is the whole of Masking Studio. You paint over the part you want changed, and everything outside the brush is left exactly as it was. Not re-rendered, not redrawn. The original file.

The alternative is restaging the whole photo, and that gives you a new room rather than a new wall. The bath moves. The tap grows a second handle. The light from the window lands somewhere else. It all looks fine until you put it next to the picture you started with.

So here is the check. Run your eye across the seam. The bath rim carries straight through it without a step, and the grout lines meet on both sides. Only the tile changes colour.

We measured it rather than trusting it: of the 118,054 pixels outside the brushed area, zero changed.

One photo. One brush. One pass.
